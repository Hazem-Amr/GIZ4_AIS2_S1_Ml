-Preprocessing in NLP

1- Stop Words high-frequency, low-semantic-value words (e.g., "the," "is," "and") removed during text preprocessing to reduce noise and computation time
(Works with sentiment analysis but not with Chatbot beacuse it will change the context os the sentence)

2- Stemming natural language processing (NLP) technique that reduces words to their base or root form (stem) by chopping off inflections, such as "-ing", "-ed", or "-s"
(Used in sentiment analysis)

3- Lemmatization is a text normalization technique in Natural Language Processing (NLP) that reduces a word to its base or dictionary form, known as a lemma.
Unlike stemming, which crudely "chops off" prefixes or suffixes to find a root, lemmatization uses a vocabulary and morphological analysis to ensure the resulting word is a valid, meaningful entry in the language. 

4- Regular Exepresion used in cleaning text

5- Part of speech categories that define the role a word plays in a sentence based on its syntax and meaning.

6- Named Entity Recognition (NER) (also known as entity extraction or chunking) is a subtask of Natural Language Processing (NLP) that identifies and classifies specific information—known as "entities"—within a text into predefined categories.
Common Entity Categories
While categories can vary by field, most systems focus on these core types: 
Person (PER): Names of real or fictional individuals (e.g., Albert Einstein, Sherlock Holmes).
Organization (ORG): Companies, institutions, or government agencies (e.g., Google, United Nations).
Location (LOC/GPE): Geographical places or geopolitical entities like cities and countries (e.g., Paris, Brazil).
Date & Time: Specific calendar dates or time periods (e.g., 5th May 2025, 3:00 PM).
Numerical Values: Quantities, percentages, or monetary sums (e.g., 10 kilograms, 50%, $100)

7- Spell correction the automated process of identifying and fixing typographical errors to improve the accuracy of text data for humans and machines alike

(Text --> tokens) mandatory --> (Stop words --> Stemming or Lemmatization --> regex --> POS --> NER --> spell correction) based on task
